01

Overview

Can corresponding outdoor images help estimate changes in air quality without relying on a scene-specific background?

Image-based air-quality estimation offers a scalable complement to stationary monitors, but models trained on a single fixed camera can learn background geometry and other location-specific cues. PairAir-Bench is a dual-location CCTV image dataset paired with synchronized air-quality measurements. The accompanying study investigates temporal visual differencing and a patch-attention model to estimate changes in particulate matter and AQI, including evaluation on an unseen location.

Research focus

Measure how well paired visual changes transfer between structurally different scenes—not just how accurately a model fits images from a familiar camera.

02

Dataset

Synchronized outdoor CCTV imagery and co-located environmental measurements from two contrasting campus locations.

LOCATION A

MT area

A built-up, traffic-influenced campus environment with fixed outdoor camera coverage.

LOCATION B

Nursery

An open, vegetation-rich environment that provides a structurally different scene for cross-location evaluation.

Synchronized labels and metadata

PM₁PM₂.₅PM₁₀AQITemperatureRelative humidity

Images are captured every 15 minutes from 07:00 to 18:45. Sensor readings are aligned to image timestamps; when an exact reading is unavailable, the paper describes using the closest reading within five minutes. The collection is described as ongoing in the paper.
